# WORKFLOW LIBRARY TAXONOMY

## Categories & Subcategories
The Operix Workflow Library is organized into the following hierarchy to support semantic search and matching.

### 1. Sales
- **Lead Generation:** Discovery, scraping, and initial capture.
- **Lead Qualification:** Scoring, ICP matching, and intent analysis.
- **Outreach:** Cold email, LinkedIn automation, and follow-up sequences.
- **Meeting Management:** Scheduling and briefing.

### 2. Marketing
- **Content Automation:** AI generation, social media posting.
- **Campaign Management:** Tracking and attribution.
- **Customer Acquisition:** Ad-to-lead pipelines.

### 3. Customer Support
- **Ticketing:** Auto-routing and categorization.
- **Knowledge Base:** AI chatbots and FAQ automation.
- **Feedback:** Sentiment analysis and routing.

### 4. Operations
- **Data Processing:** ETL, synchronization, and cleaning.
- **Reporting:** KPI dashboards and automated alerts.
- **Finance:** Invoicing and payment tracking.

### 5. AI Automation
- **Agentic Workflows:** Multi-step AI reasoning.
- **Summarization:** Call transcripts and document analysis.
- **Content Transformation:** Translation and formatting.

## Industry Applicability
Workflows are tagged with industry applicability:
- **General:** Applicable to any business.
- **Professional Services:** Law, Accounting, Consulting.
- **Healthcare:** Pharmacies, Clinics.
- **Retail/E-commerce:** Online stores, Local shops.
- **B2B SaaS:** Software companies.
